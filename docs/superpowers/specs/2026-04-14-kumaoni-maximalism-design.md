# Design Spec: Psychedelic Kumaoni Maximalism ("The Hidden Heritage")

**Topic:** Personal Portfolio Theme Change
**Date:** 2026-04-14
**Status:** Draft

## 1. Vision & Strategy
To create a "world-class" creative portfolio that honors the user's Kumaoni (Uttarakhand) heritage through a "Hidden Heritage" aesthetic. The design avoids obvious or cliché cultural symbols, instead using abstract patterns, sacred geometry, and modern psychedelic visual effects to create an immersive, regal, and trippy experience.

## 2. Visual Identity
- **Theme:** Hidden Heritage / Pahari Alchemy
- **Palette:**
  - Gerua (Deep Maroon): `#8C2D25`
  - Biswar (Creamy White): `#FFF8E1`
  - Pahadi Gold: `#D4AF37`
  - Pichora Saffron: `#E98923`
- **Typography:**
  - **Header (Hindi):** "Noto Sans Devanagari" (Weight: 900), outlined style.
  - **Body/Secondary:** "Prata" (Serif) for a regal feel.
  - **Meta/UI:** "Inter" (Sans-serif) for high-end digital precision.

## 3. Core Components
### 3.1 Background Engine
- **Liquid Gerua:** A multi-layered animated gradient that shifts between deep earthy reds.
- **Bindu Mandala:** A central, rotating engine consisting of fractal Aipan lines and radiating lotus rays. Consists of at least 3 rotating layers with different speeds.
- **Hidden Watermark:** The Kumaon Lion crest at very low opacity (3%) as a textural "easter egg."

### 3.2 Layout & Structure
- **Frame:** Subtle zig-zag geometric borders defining the top and bottom edges.
- **Hero:** Large-scale Hindi typography ("शुभम सती") centered over the mandala.
- **Navigation (Chowkies):** A symmetrical 2x2 grid of circular "buttons" using glassmorphism (background blur) and abstract Aipan motifs.
  - Resume (Vasudhara lines)
  - Workspace (Chowki star)
  - Reading List (Circular Aipan motif)
  - Contact (Geometric frame)

### 3.3 Motion & Interaction
- **Parallax:** Scrolling should reveal deeper layers of the mandala.
- **Aura Hover:** Hovering over nav items triggers a bioluminescent "cyan" glow.
- **Rhythmic Pulse:** The central mandala should subtly expand and contract (breathing effect).

## 4. Technical Implementation
- **Styles:** Vanilla CSS with heavy use of CSS Animations, Gradients, and Glassmorphism.
- **Fonts:** Google Fonts (Noto Sans Devanagari, Prata, Inter).
- **Icons:** Custom CSS-drawn Aipan motifs instead of icon libraries where possible.

## 5. Success Criteria
- The site feels "Awwwards-level" in its creative execution.
- The Kumaoni heritage is present but abstract and subtle.
- The "Psychedelic" feel is achieved through motion and depth rather than just color.
